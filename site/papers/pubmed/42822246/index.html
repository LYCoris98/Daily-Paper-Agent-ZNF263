Cohesin on the move: navigating genomic traffic during loop extrusion.

Guo, Qiao; Yuen, Karen Wing Yee; Zhang, Haoyue
Current opinion in structural biology · 2026-10-01 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文综述cohesin在环挤压中的机械调控机制，与cpcdh基因家族无直接关联。

摘要

Cohesin is a central architect of the 3D genome. As it extrudes DNA, it must navigate a 'busy' genomic landscape, leading to constant biophysical engagements with diverse chromatin binding proteins and molecular machineries. In this review, we examine how cohesin's loop extrusion is shaped by mechanical encounters with directional barriers like CCCTC-binding factor (CTCF), moving transcriptional fronts, and advancing replication forks. We highlight how these players can arrest, slow, or actively push cohesin, dynamically modulating chromatin topology. Furthermore, we discuss how the replisome traverses the cohesin ring to establish cohesion and how repair factors leverage extrusion to facilitate homology searching. Finally, we delineate the crosstalk among structural maintenance of chromosomes (SMC) motors during mitotic entry. Collectively, our review provides a comprehensive framework for these multifaceted interactions and highlights key unresolved questions, offering a future perspective on the mechanical principles of cohesin governing genome organization.

摘要翻译

黏连蛋白（cohesin）是三维基因组的核心建筑师。在挤出DNA的过程中，它必须穿越一个“繁忙”的基因组景观，导致与多种染色质结合蛋白和分子机器持续发生生物物理相互作用。在本综述中，我们探讨了黏连蛋白的环挤出如何受到与方向性屏障（如CCCTC结合因子（CTCF））、移动的转录前沿和前进的复制叉的机械性遭遇所塑造。我们重点阐述了这些因子如何能够阻止、减缓或主动推动黏连蛋白，动态调节染色质拓扑结构。此外，我们讨论了复制体（replisome）如何穿越黏连蛋白环以建立黏连，以及修复因子如何利用挤出过程促进同源搜索。最后，我们描述了有丝分裂进入期间染色体结构维持（SMC）马达之间的串扰。总体而言，我们的综述为这些多方面的相互作用提供了一个综合框架，并强调了关键未解问题，为黏连蛋白调控基因组组织的机械原理提供了未来展望。
