A YAP-TEAD-CTCF Transcriptional Regulatory Axis Associated With the Elimination of Injured Hepatocytes From the Liver.

Hatakeyama, Daiki; Okamoto-Uchida, Yoshimi; Kosaka, Misaki; Hirose, Haruka; Kofuji, Satoshi; Danzaki, Keiko; Endo, Yoshiki; Nakagawa, Michiko; Kanai, Akinori; Suzuki, Yutaka; Yokoo, Takeshi; Terai, Shuji; Shimamura, Teppei; Nishina, Hiroshi
Genes to cells : devoted to molecular & cellular mechanisms · 2026-10-05 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文研究YAP-TEAD-CTCF轴调控受损肝细胞清除的机制，与cpcdh基因家族无直接关联，但涉及转录调控和染色质重塑，可借鉴其研究思路。

摘要

The liver maintains tissue homeostasis both by undergoing regeneration and by eliminating abnormal or injured hepatocytes. The transcriptional coactivator YAP in collaboration with the TEAD family of transcription factors regulates hepatocyte proliferation. It has also been associated with injured hepatocyte elimination whereby YAP-active injured hepatocytes are extruded into the sinusoidal lumen and undergo cell death followed by macrophage phagocytosis. However, the molecular mechanisms underlying this process are unknown. To address this, we performed RNA-seq analysis in YAP-active injured hepatocytes that are eliminated from the liver. We found increased expression of multiple genes associated with cell cycle arrest and cytoskeletal remodeling. Ligand-receptor analysis using single-cell spatial transcriptomics showed that YAP-active hepatocytes express the secreted factors CSF1 and CCL2, which promote macrophage recruitment and activation. ATAC-seq analysis in YAP-active hepatocytes revealed a significant increase in chromatin accessibility at regions containing CTCF-binding motifs, and the YAP-TEAD4 complex associated with CTCF in the nucleus. These results indicate that a YAP-TEAD-CTCF axis shapes the gene expression program underlying injured hepatocyte elimination through chromatin structural remodeling, resulting in liver homeostasis.

摘要翻译

肝脏通过再生以及清除异常或受损肝细胞来维持组织稳态。转录共激活因子YAP与TEAD家族转录因子协同调控肝细胞增殖。YAP还与受损肝细胞的清除有关，其中YAP激活的受损肝细胞被挤出至窦状隙管腔，经历细胞死亡后由巨噬细胞吞噬。然而，这一过程背后的分子机制尚不清楚。为解决此问题，我们对从肝脏中被清除的YAP激活受损肝细胞进行了RNA-seq分析。我们发现多个与细胞周期停滞和细胞骨架重塑相关的基因表达增加。利用单细胞空间转录组学的配体-受体分析显示，YAP激活的肝细胞表达分泌因子CSF1和CCL2，这些因子促进巨噬细胞的招募和激活。对YAP激活肝细胞的ATAC-seq分析揭示，在含有CTCF结合基序的区域染色质可及性显著增加，且YAP-TEAD4复合物在细胞核中与CTCF相关联。这些结果表明，YAP-TEAD-CTCF轴通过染色质结构重塑塑造了受损肝细胞清除背后的基因表达程序，从而维持肝脏稳态。
