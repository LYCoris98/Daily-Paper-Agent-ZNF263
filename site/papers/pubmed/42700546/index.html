Preliminary analysis of chromatin accessibility in osteosarcoma tissues.

Ye, Fan; Hu, Po
Clinics (Sao Paulo, Brazil) · 2026-09-06 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该研究通过ATAC-seq分析骨肉瘤染色质可及性，关注AP-1家族转录因子，与cpcdh基因家族无直接关联。

摘要

Osteosarcoma (OS) is highly invasive, metastatic, and lacks effective therapies, leading to poor prognosis. This study aims to dissect the core epigenetic features of OS from the perspective of chromatin accessibility to elucidate its pathogenesis and identify novel therapeutic targets. ATAC-seq was used to map the global chromatin accessibility profiles of OS tissues versus normal controls. We identified OS-specific aberrantly accessible chromatin regions and delineated enriched transcription factor binding motifs. Key transcription factors were validated by Western blotting and flow cytometry. Notably, in regions with enhanced accessibility, the binding probability of AP-1 family members (including Atf3, Fra1, Fra2, JunB, BATF, AP-1, Jun-AP1, Bach2, and CTCF) was significantly elevated. Further analysis indicates that JunB can inhibit apoptosis of OS cells and enhance their invasive ability, suggesting that it may be related to tumor progression. Overall, JunB, as a candidate molecule with potential research value, is worthy of further verification in subsequent in vivo models and clinical samples to determine its feasibility as an intervention target for OS.

摘要翻译

骨肉瘤（Osteosarcoma, OS）具有高度侵袭性、转移性，且缺乏有效疗法，导致预后不良。本研究旨在从染色质可及性（chromatin accessibility）角度解析OS的核心表观遗传特征，以阐明其发病机制并发现新的治疗靶点。采用ATAC-seq技术绘制OS组织与正常对照的全局染色质可及性图谱。我们鉴定了OS特异性异常可及的染色质区域，并描绘了富集的转录因子结合基序。通过Western blotting和流式细胞术验证了关键转录因子。值得注意的是，在可及性增强的区域中，AP-1家族成员（包括Atf3、Fra1、Fra2、JunB、BATF、AP-1、Jun-AP1、Bach2和CTCF）的结合概率显著升高。进一步分析表明，JunB可抑制OS细胞凋亡并增强其侵袭能力，提示其可能与肿瘤进展相关。总体而言，JunB作为具有潜在研究价值的候选分子，值得在后续体内模型和临床样本中进一步验证，以确定其作为OS干预靶点的可行性。
