Layered regulation of plant 3D chromatin architecture by histone marks, transcription factors and cohesin‑mediated loops.

Su, Zezhao; Wang, Zhidan; Latrasse, David; Liu, Chang; Benhamed, Moussa
Current opinion in plant biology · 2026-10-27 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文综述植物3D染色质结构的分层调控机制，虽未直接涉及cpcdh基因家族，但其对组蛋白修饰、转录因子和cohesin介导环的讨论可为研究cpcdh家族在染色质调控中的潜在作用提供借鉴。

摘要

The three-dimensional (3D) organization of the genome has emerged as a central layer of gene regulation in eukaryotes. In plants, chromatin architecture displays both conserved features and lineage-specific characteristics, reflecting differences in genome composition, epigenetic landscapes and regulatory strategies. Recent evolutionary analyses suggest that plants preferentially adopt a chromosome-scale "global folding" strategy distinct from the compartmentalized architecture observed in animals, which raises an important question: how is regulatory information coordinated in plants in the absence of canonical architectural proteins such as CCCTC-binding factor (CTCF) and strong compartmental domains? Here, we discuss evidence supporting a model in which plant 3D genome organization arises from the interplay between three major regulatory layers: chromatin states defined by histone modifications, transcription factor-mediated regulatory interactions, and cohesin-dependent chromatin extrusion. We further propose that these mechanisms operate within a global folding framework that balances structural stability with regulatory plasticity.

摘要翻译

基因组的三维（3D）组织已成为真核生物基因调控的核心层级。在植物中，染色质结构既表现出保守特征，也展现出谱系特异性特点，反映了基因组组成、表观遗传景观和调控策略的差异。近期进化分析表明，植物倾向于采用一种染色体尺度的“全局折叠”策略，区别于动物中观察到的区室化结构，这引发了一个重要问题：在缺乏典型结构蛋白如CCCTC结合因子（CTCF）和强区室结构域的情况下，植物如何协调调控信息？在此，我们讨论了支持一种模型的证据，该模型认为植物三维基因组组织源于三个主要调控层之间的相互作用：由组蛋白修饰定义的染色质状态、转录因子介导的调控相互作用，以及黏连蛋白依赖的染色质挤压。我们进一步提出，这些机制在一种平衡结构稳定性与调控可塑性的全局折叠框架内运作。

03 研究问题

  • 具体问题：植物在缺乏 CTCF 和强区室化结构域的情况下，如何协调三维染色质组织中的调控信息？
  • 为什么重要：3D 基因组组织是真核基因调控的核心层；植物与动物在基因组架构上存在显著差异（全局折叠 vs. 区室化），理解植物特有机制对作物改良和表观遗传调控研究至关重要。
  • 现有方法为何不足：动物模型中的 CTCF/cohesin 介导 loop 挤压模型无法直接解释植物基因组中缺乏 CTCF 同源物和弱区室化的现象；早期植物 Hi-C 研究多停留在描述性层面，缺乏整合性调控框架。
  • 精确研究问题：Can plant 3D chromatin architecture be explained by the interplay of histone-mark-defined chromatin states, transcription factor-mediated interactions, and cohesin-dependent loop extrusion within a global folding framework?

06 核心思想

1) 表面方法：整合近年植物 3D 基因组学数据（Hi-C、ChIP-seq、突变体分析），提出一个三层调控模型。

2) 核心洞察：植物 3D 基因组组织不是由单一 architectural protein 驱动，而是由三个相对独立但协同的调控层共同塑造： - 第一层：组蛋白修饰（如 H3K27me3、H3K4me3）定义染色质状态，形成"预组织"的调控环境； - 第二层：转录因子介导的远程调控互作，在特定细胞类型中建立动态的增强子-启动子接触； - 第三层：cohesin 依赖的 loop 挤压，在植物中可能以更局部、更动态的方式运作。

这三层机制在"全局折叠"框架内协同，实现结构稳定性与调控可塑性的平衡。

3) 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：在缺乏经典 architectural protein 的系统中，3D 基因组组织可能由多层冗余机制协同驱动，而非依赖单一主导因子。这一"多层冗余"原则可能适用于其他非模式真核生物，也可为研究特定基因家族（如 cpdch）在染色质空间中的调控角色提供框架。

以下为可迁移至 cpdch 基因家族研究的概念、方法和设计思路：

  1. 多层调控框架：本文提出的"组蛋白修饰定义状态 → 转录因子建立互作 → cohesin 稳定结构"三层模型，可迁移至 cpdch 基因家族研究。若 cpdch 编码的蛋白参与染色质调控，可检验其作用于哪一层（如是否影响组蛋白修饰状态、是否介导远程互作、是否与 cohesin 通路交叉）。

  2. "全局折叠"视角：在研究 cpdch 基因家族时，不应仅关注局部启动子/增强子互作，而应考虑其在染色体尺度折叠中的角色。可设计 Hi-C 实验比较 cpdch 突变体与野生型的全局互作矩阵差异。

  3. 缺乏经典 architectural protein 的替代机制：植物缺乏 CTCF 的教训提示，研究 cpdch 基因家族时不应预设其必须通过已知的动物机制发挥作用，而应开放探索植物特有的调控策略。

  5. 结构稳定性与调控可塑性的平衡：这一概念可用于理解 cpdch 基因家族在发育或环境响应中的双重角色——既维持基因组结构稳定，又允许特定基因的灵活调控。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract addresses a genuinely interesting and timely question in plant chromatin biology, namely how regulatory information is coordinated in the apparent absence of CTCF and strong compartmental domains. The three-layer model is conceptually clear and potentially useful as an organizing framework. However, from the supplied material alone, the manuscript reads as a synthesis and opinion piece whose central model is asserted rather than demonstrated. No specific evidence, no cited examples, and no mechanistic detail are visible, so the strength of the underlying support cannot be evaluated. The abstract is well written and accessible, but the case for the model remains unestablished from what is provided.

  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Plant molecular biologists, epigenetics researchers, and genome-architecture scientists interested in how 3D organization is established without canonical architectural proteins. Also relevant to evolutionary biologists comparing plant and animal chromatin strategies, and to researchers working on cohesin, histone marks, or transcription factor function in gene regulation.

  • Major strengths

  • The central question is well posed and biologically meaningful, namely how regulatory information is coordinated in plants lacking CTCF and strong compartmental domains.
  • The three-layer framework (histone marks, transcription factors, cohesin loops) is a clear and testable conceptual model.
  • The abstract is concise and readable for a broad audience.

  • The framing around a "global folding" strategy versus animal compartmentalization is a useful comparative angle.
