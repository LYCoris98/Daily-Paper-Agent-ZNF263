Integrative analysis reveals extensive interactions among C2H2 zinc finger proteins at chromatin loop anchors.

Radovani, Ernest; Nabeel-Shah, Syed; Marcon, Edyta; Pu, Shuye; Zhong, Guoqing; Tang, Hua; Guo, Hongbo; Kaplow, Irene M; Emili, Andrew; Hughes, Timothy R; Greenblatt, Jack F
Nucleic acids research · 2026-10-19 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文研究C2H2锌指蛋白在染色质环锚点的相互作用，与cpcdh基因家族无关。

摘要

Cys2-His2-zinc-finger proteins (C2H2-ZFPs) form the largest class of human transcription factors, yet their potential roles in higher-order genome organization remain poorly defined. To date, only a few C2H2-ZFPs, e.g. CTCF, have been implicated in chromatin organization. Here, we systematically examined the global relationship between C2H2-ZFPs and long-range chromatin interactions (LRIs). By integrating ChIP-seq datasets for 216 C2H2-ZFPs with genome-wide chromatin interaction maps, we find that >40% of human C2H2-ZFPs are significantly enriched at LRI anchors. Depletion of a subset of such C2H2-ZFPs is associated with altered expression of genes linked by the LRIs they occupy. To investigate whether protein-protein interactions (PPIs) contribute to this pattern, we generated a large-scale interactome using affinity purification-mass spectrometry, encompassing 345 C2H2-ZFPs, and LUMIER binary interaction assays for 204 C2H2-ZFPs, revealing extensive connectivity among the family members. Integrative analysis of PPIs, ChIP-seq, and chromatin interaction datasets revealed that interacting C2H2-ZFP pairs are significantly co-enriched at LRIs and frequently localize to the same or opposing loop anchors. Finally, we show that approximately 35% of LRI-associated C2H2-ZFP binding sites overlap somatic mutations in cancer genomes. Together, our results reveal a widespread network of C2H2-ZFP interactions at chromatin loop anchors, providing an important resource for understanding chromatin organization.

摘要翻译

Cys2-His2-锌指蛋白（C2H2-ZFPs）构成人类转录因子中最大的一类，然而它们在高级基因组组织中的潜在作用仍鲜为人知。迄今为止，仅有少数C2H2-ZFPs，例如CTCF，被牵涉到染色质组织中。在此，我们系统性地考察了C2H2-ZFPs与长程染色质相互作用（LRIs）之间的全局关系。通过将216个C2H2-ZFPs的ChIP-seq数据集与全基因组染色质相互作用图谱整合，我们发现超过40%的人类C2H2-ZFPs在LRI锚点处显著富集。部分此类C2H2-ZFPs的缺失与它们所占据的LRIs连接的基因表达改变相关。为探究蛋白质-蛋白质相互作用（PPIs）是否促成这一模式，我们利用亲和纯化-质谱法生成了一个涵盖345个C2H2-ZFPs的大规模相互作用组，并对204个C2H2-ZFPs进行了LUMIER二元相互作用测定，揭示了家族成员间的广泛连接性。对PPIs、ChIP-seq和染色质相互作用数据集的整合分析显示，相互作用的C2H2-ZFP对在LRIs处显著共富集，并经常定位于相同或相对的环锚点。最后，我们表明约35%的LRI相关C2H2-ZFP结合位点与癌症基因组中的体细胞突变重叠。综合来看，我们的结果揭示了染色质环锚点处C2H2-ZFP相互作用的广泛网络，为理解染色质组织提供了重要资源。
