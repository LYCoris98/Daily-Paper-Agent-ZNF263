Placental DNA Methylation of HSD11B2 and IGF2 Is Associated With Fetal Growth in the Cebu Longitudinal Health and Nutrition Survey.

Klibaner-Schiff, Eleanor; Rutherford, Julienne; deMartelly, Victoria; Lagardo, Fe; Gundling, William; Wildman, Derek E; Lee, Nanette R; Kuzawa, Christopher
American journal of human biology : the official journal of the Human Biology Council · 2026-09-30 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文研究胎盘HSD11B2和IGF2的DNA甲基化与胎儿生长的关联，与cpcdh基因家族无关。

摘要

OBJECTIVES: The intrauterine environment exerts lasting impacts on fetal growth and development, with potential long-term effects on later health. We examined whether placental DNA methylation at HSD11B2 and IGF2-two key regulators of the intrauterine environment-is associated with fetal growth among participants in the Cebu Longitudinal Health and Nutrition Survey (CLHNS). METHODS: We analyzed DNA methylation in placental samples from 19 births in the CLHNS using the Illumina Infinium HumanMethylation450 BeadChip. Focusing on CpG sites within HSD11B2 and the IGF2/H19 imprinting control region (ICR), we used linear regression models adjusted for gestational age to assess associations of DNA methylation with birth weight, head circumference, length, and neonatal adiposity. RESULTS: Higher methylation at three CpG sites within HSD11B2 was significantly associated with reduced fetal growth: one site was associated with reduced birth weight and head circumference, and two sites were associated with shorter birth length. Within the IGF2/H19 ICR, two CpG sites were significantly associated with both increased birth weight and head circumference. Both CpGs overlapped with predicted CCCTC-binding factor (CTCF) binding sites, consistent with known regulatory mechanisms linking increased methylation at the ICR to increased IGF2 expression. CONCLUSIONS: These findings support the hypothesis that DNA methylation at regulatory regions of HSD11B2 and IGF2 contributes to variation in fetal growth, linking placental epigenetic patterns to birth outcomes.

摘要翻译

目的：宫内环境对胎儿生长发育具有持久影响，并可能对后期健康产生长期效应。我们考察了参与宿务纵向健康与营养调查（CLHNS）的个体中，胎盘HSD11B2和IGF2（这两个是宫内环境的关键调节因子）的DNA甲基化是否与胎儿生长相关。方法：我们使用Illumina Infinium HumanMethylation450 BeadChip分析了CLHNS中19例出生个体的胎盘样本DNA甲基化。聚焦于HSD11B2和IGF2/H19印记控制区（ICR）内的CpG位点，我们使用调整了孕周的线性回归模型来评估DNA甲基化与出生体重、头围、身长和新生儿肥胖度的关联。结果：HSD11B2内三个CpG位点的较高甲基化与胎儿生长减少显著相关：一个位点与出生体重和头围减少相关，两个位点与出生身长较短相关。在IGF2/H19 ICR内，两个CpG位点与出生体重和头围增加均显著相关。这两个CpG位点均与预测的CCCTC结合因子（CTCF）结合位点重叠，这与已知的调控机制一致，即ICR甲基化增加与IGF2表达增加相关。结论：这些发现支持了HSD11B2和IGF2调控区域DNA甲基化有助于胎儿生长变异的假说，将胎盘表观遗传模式与出生结局联系起来。
