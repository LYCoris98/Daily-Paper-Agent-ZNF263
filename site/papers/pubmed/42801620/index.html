GSLR Drives Lung Adenocarcinoma as a Critical Regulator of Energy Homeostasis Through Creatine Kinase B Activation.

Qian, Xinyi; Yang, Juze; Ren, Jiayi; Mao, Xiaowei; Li, Jia; Sun, Tengfei; Dong, Liangliang; Chen, Enguo; Lu, Yan; Liu, Pengyuan
Advanced science (Weinheim, Baden-Wurttemberg, Germany) · 2026-09-27 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文研究lncRNA GSLR通过CKB调控肺癌能量代谢，与cpcdh基因家族无关。

摘要

Lung cancer remains the leading cause of cancer-related mortality, with extremely high energy demands during progression. Long non-coding RNAs (lncRNAs) have emerged as crucial regulators in cancer metabolism; however, their role in reprogramming energy metabolism in lung cancer remains incompletely understood. In this study, we identify a glucose/glutamine sensitive lncRNA, GSLR, as a driver of lung adenocarcinoma (LUAD) progression. GSLR is markedly upregulated in LUAD, and its high expression is associated with poor patient prognosis. Nutrient restriction reduces chromatin accessibility at the GSLR locus via altered histone marks, whereas CTCF activates its transcription. Mechanistically, GSLR directly binds the RNA helicase DHX9 and recruits it to the creatine kinase B (CKB) promoter, arresting R-loop accumulation and thereby promoting CKB transcription. Elevated CKB expression sustains intracellular ATP homeostasis, thereby stabilizing mitochondrial membrane potential, preventing calcium overload, and reducing reactive oxygen species (ROS) accumulation. Collectively, our findings identify GSLR as a novel regulator of energy homeostasis that promotes lung cancer progression through the GSLR/DHX9/CKB axis. Targeting GSLR may thus represent a promising therapeutic strategy for LUAD.

摘要翻译

肺癌仍然是癌症相关死亡的主要原因，其进展过程中具有极高的能量需求。长链非编码RNA（lncRNAs）已成为癌症代谢中的关键调控因子；然而，它们在肺癌能量代谢重编程中的作用仍未被完全理解。在本研究中，我们鉴定了一个葡萄糖/谷氨酰胺敏感的lncRNA，GSLR，作为肺腺癌（LUAD）进展的驱动因子。GSLR在LUAD中显著上调，其高表达与患者不良预后相关。营养限制通过改变组蛋白标记降低GSLR位点的染色质可及性，而CTCF则激活其转录。机制上，GSLR直接结合RNA解旋酶DHX9，并将其招募至肌酸激酶B（CKB）启动子，阻止R-loop积累，从而促进CKB转录。CKB表达升高维持细胞内ATP稳态，进而稳定线粒体膜电位，防止钙超载，并减少活性氧（ROS）积累。总体而言，我们的发现将GSLR鉴定为能量稳态的新型调控因子，通过GSLR/DHX9/CKB轴促进肺癌进展。因此，靶向GSLR可能代表一种有前景的LUAD治疗策略。
