PDS5A and TOP2B cooperate for chromatin recruitment via CTCF.

Gonzalez-Buendia, Edgar; Ortabozkoyun, Havva; Reinberg, Danny
Science advances · 2026-10-30 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该文研究PDS5A与TOP2B在CTCF位点的协同作用，与cpcdh基因家族无关。

摘要

PDS5A, a cohesin regulatory subunit, and topoisomerase IIB (TOP2B), an enzyme resolving DNA topological problems, interact with CTCF and regulate transcription and genome organization. We ascertained that PDS5A and TOP2B are recruited to CTCF-bound chromatin cooperatively and that catalytically active TOP2B increases PDS5A occupancy genome wide. Notably, the amino-terminal 95 to 116 amino acids of CTCF interacts with PDS5A, giving rise to CTCF-PDS5A-TOP2B interaction in vitro and to PDS5A chromatin enrichment via active TOP2B in vivo. PDS5A-TOP2B function together in moderating the resultant gene expression and genome organization. Loss of CTCF (95 to 116 amino acids) led to dysregulated gene expression and mildly reduced chromatin loops. In gliomas, PDS5A and TOP2B expression levels vary, but correlate, contributing to distinctive gene expression patterns. Inducible PDS5A knockdown led to reduced TOP2B occupancy and altered gene expression. Furthermore, PDS5A mediated sensitivity to TOP2 cancer drugs in glioma cells. This functional interaction between PDS5A and TOP2B at CTCF boundaries expands upon the mechanisms affecting gene regulation through genome organization, with implications for glioma therapeutics.

摘要翻译

PDS5A，一种黏连蛋白调节亚基，以及拓扑异构酶IIB（TOP2B），一种解决DNA拓扑问题的酶，与CTCF相互作用并调节转录和基因组组织。我们确认PDS5A和TOP2B被协同招募至CTCF结合的染色质上，且具有催化活性的TOP2B在全基因组范围内增加PDS5A的占据率。值得注意的是，CTCF的氨基末端第95至116个氨基酸与PDS5A相互作用，在体外形成CTCF-PDS5A-TOP2B复合物，并在体内通过活性TOP2B促进PDS5A在染色质上的富集。PDS5A-TOP2B共同作用以调节由此产生的基因表达和基因组组织。CTCF（第95至116个氨基酸）的缺失导致基因表达失调和染色质环轻度减少。在胶质瘤中，PDS5A和TOP2B的表达水平存在差异，但相互关联，促成独特的基因表达模式。诱导性PDS5A敲低导致TOP2B占据率降低和基因表达改变。此外，PDS5A介导了胶质瘤细胞对TOP2癌症药物的敏感性。PDS5A与TOP2B在CTCF边界处的这种功能性相互作用扩展了通过基因组组织影响基因调控的机制，对胶质瘤治疗具有潜在意义。
