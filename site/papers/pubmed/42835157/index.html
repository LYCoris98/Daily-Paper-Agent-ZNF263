Integrated single-cell and multi-cohort analyses reveal CTCF as a key regulator of chemo-immunotherapy resistance in lung adenocarcinoma.

Chen, Hui; Zhang, Taiyang; Zhang, Yiwei; Li, Mohan; Yang, Haoran; Zhang, Yue; Xu, Feng
Frontiers in cell and developmental biology · 2026-10-06 · 全文(pmc) · 原文

该研究聚焦CTCF在肺腺癌化疗免疫治疗耐药中的作用，与cpcdh基因家族无直接关联。

摘要

BACKGROUND: Chemo-immunotherapy has improved clinical outcomes in non-small cell lung cancer (NSCLC), but resistance remains a major challenge. The tumor cell states and molecular mechanisms underlying treatment resistance are not fully understood. METHODS: Single-cell RNA sequencing data from NSCLC patients receiving chemo-immunotherapy were integrated with public transcriptomic cohorts and functional experiments. Resistance-associated tumor cell populations were identified, and key regulatory factors were investigated through bioinformatic analyses and experimental validation. RESULTS: Single-cell analysis identified a malignant epithelial cell subpopulation enriched in patients with stable disease, suggesting a resistance-associated tumor cell state. Genes derived from this population were associated with unfavorable prognosis and were used to construct a prognostic model with consistent performance across multiple independent cohorts. Among the candidate genes, CTCF was identified as a key regulator associated with poor survival and reduced immune infiltration. In an orthotopic lung tumor model, Ctcf knockdown enhanced the antitumor efficacy of pemetrexed combined with anti-PD-1 treatment and promoted CD8(+) T-cell activity. Mechanistically, CTCF directly upregulated RRM2 and CD276, which mediated chemotherapy resistance and immune suppression, respectively. CONCLUSION: Our findings identify CTCF as a critical regulator of chemo-immunotherapy resistance in NSCLC. The CTCF-RRM2/CD276 axis links tumor-intrinsic drug resistance with immune evasion and may represent a potential therapeutic target.

摘要翻译

背景：化学免疫治疗改善了非小细胞肺癌（NSCLC）的临床结局，但耐药性仍是主要挑战。肿瘤细胞状态及治疗耐药背后的分子机制尚未完全阐明。方法：将接受化学免疫治疗的NSCLC患者的单细胞RNA测序数据与公共转录组队列及功能实验相结合。鉴定了与耐药相关的肿瘤细胞群体，并通过生物信息学分析和实验验证研究了关键调控因子。结果：单细胞分析识别出一个在病情稳定患者中富集的恶性上皮细胞亚群，提示存在与耐药相关的肿瘤细胞状态。源自该群体的基因与不良预后相关，并用于构建预后模型，该模型在多个独立队列中表现一致。在候选基因中，CTCF被确定为与较差生存率和降低的免疫浸润相关的关键调控因子。在原位肺肿瘤模型中，Ctcf敲低增强了培美曲塞联合抗PD-1治疗的抗肿瘤疗效，并促进了CD8(+) T细胞活性。机制上，CTCF直接上调RRM2和CD276，分别介导化疗耐药和免疫抑制。结论：我们的发现将CTCF确定为NSCLC化学免疫治疗耐药的关键调控因子。CTCF-RRM2/CD276轴将肿瘤内在药物耐药与免疫逃逸联系起来，可能代表一个潜在的治疗靶点。
