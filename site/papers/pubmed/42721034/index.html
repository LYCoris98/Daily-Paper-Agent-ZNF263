Selective control of HLA-DRB1 and HLA-DQA1 transcription through DR/DQ super enhancer microelements.

Majumder, Parimal; Lee, Joshua; Butler, Royce; Scharer, Christopher D; Boss, Jeremy M
Cell reports · 2026-09-10 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文研究MHC-II基因的超级增强子微元件调控，与cpcdh基因家族无关。

摘要

MHC-II gene expression requires promoter-proximal and distal organizing elements. A super enhancer located between the HLA-DR and -DQ genes was dissected into five microdomains (regions A-E) to define its mechanism of action. Regions A and B were required for maximal expression of HLA-DQA1. Region A facilitated HLA-DQA1 expression and substituted for region B in its absence, as well as mediating longer-range interactions with distal CTCF sites. Region D modulated HLA-DRB1 through specific 3D chromatin interactions, whereas a local insulator element (XL9) broadly interacted with the SE. Through deletion of HLA-DRB1 proximal-promoter elements, HLA-DQ expression increased by co-opting interactions with region D, indicating that competition between microelements regulates the absolute levels of MHC-II genes. Together, these data define an additional layer of control for MHC-II genes encoded in one of the most polymorphic and disease-associated regions of the human genome.

摘要翻译

MHC-II基因表达需要启动子近端和远端的组织元件。位于HLA-DR和-DQ基因之间的一个超级增强子被解析为五个微结构域（区域A-E），以定义其作用机制。区域A和B是HLA-DQA1最大表达所必需的。区域A促进HLA-DQA1表达，并在区域B缺失时替代其功能，同时介导与远端CTCF位点的更长程相互作用。区域D通过特定的3D染色质相互作用调节HLA-DRB1，而一个局部绝缘子元件（XL9）与SE广泛相互作用。通过删除HLA-DRB1近端启动子元件，HLA-DQ表达通过共选区域D的相互作用而增加，表明微元件之间的竞争调节MHC-II基因的绝对水平。综合来看，这些数据定义了人类基因组中最多态性和疾病相关区域之一编码的MHC-II基因的额外调控层次。
