Exploring long noncoding RNAs as a molecular connection between immune response and autism spectrum disorder.

Roschyk, Anaya; Campbell, Daniel B
Frontiers in toxicology · 2026-10-31 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文研究长链非编码RNA在自闭症免疫失调中的作用，与cpcdh基因家族无关。

摘要

Autism spectrum disorder (ASD) is a neurodevelopmental condition increasingly linked to immune dysregulation, with long noncoding RNAs (lncRNAs) emerging as potential regulators of both neural and immune processes. This study commenced by analyzing ten unique lncRNAs and integrated ASD whole-blood datasets using the online platform GTex to identify expression of lncRNAs in various tissues. Moreover, the platform Network Analyst was utilized to assess differentially expressed lncRNAs and associated transcriptomic networks. A total of 93 upregulated and 51 downregulated lncRNAs were identified in people with ASD with tissue specific enrichment observed for MSNP1AS and LNC01238 in immune related systems. The transcription factor CEBPA was shown to interact with the upregulated lncRNA MALAT1, which influenced immune cell differentiation, inflammation and cytokine signaling. Intriguingly CEBPA simultaneously interacted with downregulated lnc RNA MIR22HG. Comparatively, downregulated lncRNAs were associated with the transcription factors TEAD2, CEBPA, CTCF, E2F4, TFAP2C, HIF1A, ETS1, HNF4A, SNAPC4, STAT1, STAT2, TEAD2, FOXC1, BRCA1, AR, E2F4, EOMES, SPI1, DACH1, E2F1, TP63, HMAP1, KLF9, HDGF, and MAZ. Overall, the findings support a role for lncRNAs in ASD pathophysiology and immune dysregulation, pointing to their potential as biomarkers and potential therapeutic targets for future research.

摘要翻译

自闭症谱系障碍（ASD）是一种神经发育状况，日益与免疫失调相关联，而长链非编码RNA（lncRNAs）正逐渐被视为神经和免疫过程的潜在调节因子。本研究首先分析了十个独特的lncRNAs，并利用在线平台GTex整合了ASD全血数据集，以识别lncRNAs在不同组织中的表达情况。此外，利用Network Analyst平台评估了差异表达的lncRNAs及其相关的转录组网络。在ASD患者中，共鉴定出93个上调lncRNAs和51个下调lncRNAs，其中MSNP1AS和LNC01238在免疫相关系统中表现出组织特异性富集。转录因子CEBPA被显示与上调的lncRNA MALAT1相互作用，该相互作用影响了免疫细胞分化、炎症和细胞因子信号传导。有趣的是，CEBPA同时与下调的lncRNA MIR22HG相互作用。相比之下，下调的lncRNAs与转录因子TEAD2、CEBPA、CTCF、E2F4、TFAP2C、HIF1A、ETS1、HNF4A、SNAPC4、STAT1、STAT2、TEAD2、FOXC1、BRCA1、AR、E2F4、EOMES、SPI1、DACH1、E2F1、TP63、HMAP1、KLF9、HDGF和MAZ相关联。总体而言，这些发现支持lncRNAs在ASD病理生理学和免疫失调中的作用，指出它们作为生物标志物和未来研究潜在治疗靶点的可能性。
