Chromatin accessibility predictions reveal lineage-structured effects of noncoding variants across hematopoiesis

Kazemi M; Morris JA
bioRxiv 预印本 · 2026-11-13 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文用ChromBPNet预测造血分化中非编码变异对染色质可及性的影响，虽涉及基因调控但未聚焦cpcdh基因家族，仅可借鉴其变异效应预测方法。

摘要

<h4>Background: </h4> Noncoding variants account for over 90% of genome-wide association study (GWAS) findings, but their mechanisms are often unclear. Sequence-to-function models provide one approach to variant interpretation by predicting the effects of genetic variation on molecular phenotypes. ChromBPNet predicts variant effects on chromatin accessibility, but how these predictions are organized across differentiation hierarchies and relate to orthogonal predictions remains unclear. Results. We trained ChromBPNet models for 17 hematopoietic cell types and scored 302,830 variants. Of 242,307 variants accessible in at least one cell type, 5.6% were predicted to alter chromatin accessibility. Variants active in more cell types had larger predicted effects. Predicted effects also reflected hematopoietic lineage structure, with sharing declining with lineage distance while effect direction was preserved in 99.6% of parent-child comparisons. ChromBPNet predictions agreed with GWAS fine-mapping but largely identified distinct variants. Fine-mapping-only variants were enriched for coding and transcript-associated variation, whereas ChromBPNet-only variants were enriched for regulatory elements, accessible chromatin, and trait-relevant activity. In K562 CRISPRi datasets, predicted accessibility gain showed limited association with target-gene validation. Regulatory elements containing CTCF motifs were less likely to have validated target genes, consistent with a role in chromatin organization rather than gene activation. Conclusions. ChromBPNet and fine-mapping provide complementary information about variant function. Predicted accessibility effects identify the cell types in which noncoding variants are likely to act and capture relationships across the hematopoietic hierarchy, but provide limited information about the genes they regulate. All predictions are available at: https://bloodnnet.ccbr.utoronto.ca/.

摘要翻译

<h4>背景：</h4> 非编码变异占全基因组关联研究（GWAS）发现的90%以上，但其机制往往尚不明确。序列到功能模型通过预测遗传变异对分子表型的影响，为变异解读提供了一种方法。ChromBPNet预测变异对染色质可及性的影响，但这些预测如何在不同分化层级中组织，以及它们与正交预测的关系仍不清楚。结果：我们为17种造血细胞类型训练了ChromBPNet模型，并对302,830个变异进行了评分。在至少一种细胞类型中可及的242,307个变异中，5.6%被预测会改变染色质可及性。在更多细胞类型中活跃的变异具有更大的预测效应。预测效应也反映了造血谱系结构，共享程度随谱系距离增加而下降，而效应方向在99.6%的亲子比较中得以保留。ChromBPNet预测与GWAS精细定位一致，但主要识别出不同的变异。仅精细定位的变异富集于编码和转录相关变异，而仅ChromBPNet的变异富集于调控元件、可及染色质和性状相关活性。在K562 CRISPRi数据集中，预测的可及性增加与靶基因验证的关联有限。含有CTCF基序的调控元件不太可能有已验证的靶基因，这与它们在染色质组织而非基因激活中的作用一致。结论：ChromBPNet和精细定位提供了关于变异功能的互补信息。预测的可及性效应识别了非编码变异可能作用的细胞类型，并捕获了造血层级中的关系，但对其调控的基因提供的信息有限。所有预测可在以下网址获取：https://bloodnnet.ccbr.utoronto.ca/。
