Local chromatin, 3D genome folding and transposon cargo shape Sleeping Beauty transposition

Moene CJ; Eder M; Dauban L; Yáñez-Cuna JO; Kocher AA; Eschauzier L; Verkuilen M; Franceschini-Santos VH; van Steensel B
bioRxiv 预印本 · 2026-11-03 · 全文(europepmc) · 原文

该文研究Sleeping Beauty转座子的转座机制，与cpcdh基因家族无关。

摘要

The cut-and-paste DNA transposon Sleeping Beauty (SB) is widely used for genome engineering, mutagenesis and gene delivery. When mobilized from a genomic donor position (“launch site”), SB preferentially reintegrates locally (< 2 Mb), but factors governing its transposition from genomic sites remain incompletely understood. Here we analysed local transposition experiments in mouse embryonic stem cells to determine the influence of chromatin context and transposon cargo on SB transposition. Transposons in launch sites with transcriptionally active, accessible chromatin showed lower transposition efficiency. Large cargo and cargo with higher transcriptional activity also result in reduced transposition efficiency. After excision, local reintegration patterns closely resembled 3D contact frequencies of the launch site. This indicates that chromosome folding is a major determinant of the transposition pattern. Surprisingly, we did not detect any effect of the destination chromatin composition on reintegration efficiency. Cargo sequence modified the reintegration patterns: promoter-containing cargo increased both very proximal (<10kb) and interchromosomal transpositions, while CTCF binding sites in the cargo increased reintegration on the launch chromosome. Together, these results define key determinants of SB transposition in chromosomes and inform the design of SB-based genome engineering strategies.

摘要翻译

切割-粘贴型DNA转座子睡美人（Sleeping Beauty, SB）广泛用于基因组工程、诱变和基因递送。当从基因组供体位点（“发射位点”）动员时，SB优先在局部（< 2 Mb）重新整合，但控制其从基因组位点转座的因素仍不完全清楚。在此，我们分析了小鼠胚胎干细胞中的局部转座实验，以确定染色质环境和转座子货物对SB转座的影响。位于具有转录活性、可及染色质的发射位点中的转座子表现出较低的转座效率。大尺寸货物和具有较高转录活性的货物也导致转座效率降低。切除后，局部重新整合模式与发射位点的三维接触频率高度相似。这表明染色体折叠是转座模式的主要决定因素。令人惊讶的是，我们未检测到目的地染色质组成对重新整合效率的任何影响。货物序列改变了重新整合模式：含启动子的货物增加了极近端（<10kb）和染色体间转座，而货物中的CTCF结合位点增加了在发射染色体上的重新整合。综合这些结果，定义了染色体中SB转座的关键决定因素，并为基于SB的基因组工程策略设计提供了信息。
