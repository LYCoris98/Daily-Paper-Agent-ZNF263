DNMT1-Mediated Epigenetic Reprogramming Drives KMT2A Amplifications and Rearrangements

Lineberger MA; Gray ZH; Ferman BI; O’Donnell E; Smith RG; Schiela K; Udara A; Chetal K; Azadegan C; Toma MM; Sumner E; Santoro J; Miranda C; Ahmed M; Wilson J; Lautert-Dutra W; Li C; McKelvey A; Kaniskan HÜ; Zhang W; Jin J; Puddu F; Consugar M; Walsh M; Bellacosa A; Duy C; Lee H; Skorski T; Liu Y; Sadreyev RI; Whetstine JR
bioRxiv 预印本 · 2026-09-22 · 全文(europepmc) · 原文

该文研究DNMT1介导的表观遗传重编程驱动KMT2A扩增和重排，与cpcdh基因家族无直接关联。

摘要

<h4>ABSTRACT</h4> MLL/KMT2A amplifications and rearrangements are prevalent in infant, adult, and therapy-induced leukemia; however, the molecular contributors controlling these alterations remain elusive. Here, we reveal that rapid CTCF degradation generates local genome structure alterations at KMT2A and copy gains and rearrangements. We then established a conserved, coordinated interplay between DNA and histone methylation pathways that control CTCF occupancy, and in turn, KMT2A locus stability. For example, DNMT1 overexpression promotes increased H3K9 methylation, reduced CTCF occupancy, and causes KMT2A alterations. However, DNMT1 inhibition suppresses these events and KMT2A alterations caused by topoisomerase II inhibition. Locus-specific epigenome targeting demonstrated that DNA or H3K9 methylation promotes KMT2A copy gains and rearrangement, whereas targeted TET activity suppresses these events upon methylation perturbation or doxorubicin treatment. These findings identify a conserved, coordinated DNA–histone methylation axis governing KMT2A amplifications and rearrangement susceptibility, revealing biomarkers and therapeutic targets to predict and intercept these events in cancer. <h4>STATEMENT OF SIGNIFICANCE</h4> This study reveals a conserved DNA-histone methylation mechanism controlling the susceptibility of the KMT2A locus to copy gain and rearrange. These events can be generated or prevented by genetic, therapeutic or epigenome editing. Furthermore, DNMT1 inhibition blocks chemotherapy-induced KMT2A disruption, identifying a therapeutic strategy to intercept these oncogenic genomic alterations.

摘要翻译

<h4>摘要</h4> MLL/KMT2A扩增和重排常见于婴儿、成人及治疗相关白血病中；然而，控制这些改变的分子因素仍不明确。在此，我们揭示快速CTCF降解会在KMT2A位点产生局部基因组结构改变以及拷贝数增加和重排。随后，我们建立了DNA和组蛋白甲基化通路之间保守的协调相互作用，该作用控制CTCF占据，进而影响KMT2A位点稳定性。例如，DNMT1过表达促进H3K9甲基化增加、CTCF占据减少，并导致KMT2A改变。然而，DNMT1抑制可抑制这些事件以及由拓扑异构酶II抑制引起的KMT2A改变。位点特异性表观基因组靶向表明，DNA或H3K9甲基化促进KMT2A拷贝数增加和重排，而靶向TET活性在甲基化扰动或多柔比星处理下抑制这些事件。这些发现识别了一个保守的、协调的DNA-组蛋白甲基化轴，调控KMT2A扩增和重排易感性，揭示了预测和拦截癌症中这些事件的生物标志物和治疗靶点。 <h4>意义声明</h4> 本研究揭示了一种保守的DNA-组蛋白甲基化机制，控制KMT2A位点对拷贝数增加和重排的易感性。这些事件可通过遗传、治疗或表观基因组编辑产生或预防。此外，DNMT1抑制可阻断化疗诱导的KMT2A破坏，识别了一种拦截这些致癌基因组改变的治疗策略。
