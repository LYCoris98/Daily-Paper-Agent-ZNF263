NAD Metabolism Drives CTCF-Cohesin Chromatin Regulation in EBV-Transformed B-Cells

Maestri D; Sklutuis R; Caruso LB; Preston-Alp S; Gatto M; Soldan SS; Park P; Melick L; Corveleyn L; Sidoli S; Kossenkov A; Goldman AR; Zhang W; Tang H; Napoletani G; Liao Y; Lieberman PM; Gewurz BE; Tempera I
bioRxiv 预印本 · 2026-11-02 · 全文(europepmc) · 原文

该文研究NAD代谢通过PARP1调控CTCF/cohesin染色质结构，与cpcdh基因家族无直接关联，但涉及染色质调控机制可借鉴。

摘要

<h4>Summary</h4> Chromatin architecture regulates gene expression, and it is maintained by architectural proteins such as CTCF and the cohesin complex. In Epstein–Barr virus infected cells, viral chromatin organization is stabilized through the interaction between PARP1 and CTCF, ensuring maintenance of latent gene expression. EBV⁺ B cells exhibit elevated PARP1 activity, which imposes a substantial metabolic burden due to NAD⁺ consumption by PARP1 itself. We demonstrate that efficient EBV infection and B-cell transformation rely on the NAD salvage pathway to sustain PARP1 activity and CTCF-cohesin chromatin folding. Pharmacologic inhibition of the rate-limiting enzyme NAMPT with FK866 phenocopies the chromatin architectural disruption induced by PARP1 and changes in NAD levels affects CTCF/cohesin chromatin architectural remodeling and loop extrusion. Moreover, NAMPT inhibition reduces tumor burden in EBV⁺ xenograft models. These findings identify a critical metabolic dependency linking NAD⁺ homeostasis to CTCF/cohesin mediated chromatin architecture and reveal a therapeutic vulnerability in EBV-associated malignancies.

摘要翻译

<h4>摘要</h4> 染色质结构调控基因表达，并由CTCF和cohesin复合物等结构蛋白维持。在Epstein–Barr病毒感染的细胞中，病毒染色质组织通过PARP1与CTCF之间的相互作用得以稳定，确保潜伏基因表达的维持。EBV⁺ B细胞表现出升高的PARP1活性，这因PARP1自身消耗NAD⁺而带来显著的代谢负担。我们证明，高效的EBV感染和B细胞转化依赖于NAD补救途径以维持PARP1活性和CTCF-cohesin染色质折叠。使用FK866对限速酶NAMPT的药理学抑制，模拟了PARP1诱导的染色质结构破坏，且NAD水平的变化影响CTCF/cohesin染色质结构重塑和环挤出。此外，NAMPT抑制在EBV⁺异种移植模型中减少了肿瘤负担。这些发现确定了一种关键的代谢依赖性，将NAD⁺稳态与CTCF/cohesin介导的染色质结构联系起来，并揭示了EBV相关恶性肿瘤中的治疗脆弱性。
