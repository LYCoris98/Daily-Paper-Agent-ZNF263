Evolution of CTCF underlies the establishment of loop extrusion as a predominant chromatin folding mechanism in vertebrates

Astica L; Acemel RD; Schwarzenbacher H; Racioppi C; Rojas AM; Rotllant J; Bogdanovic O; Real FM; Christiaen L; Zinzen RP; Lupianez DG
bioRxiv 预印本 · 2026-10-23 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文研究CTCF蛋白在脊椎动物中的进化及其对染色质折叠机制的影响，与cpcdh基因家族无直接关联。

摘要

CTCF is a DNA-binding protein that emerged in the last bilaterian ancestor and that plays a central role in 3D genome organization. Yet, despite its relevance, genomic studies have revealed notable differences in CTCF function across animal clades. In vertebrates, CTCF is essential for TAD organization through cohesin-mediated loop extrusion, whereas its contribution to chromatin organization outside this clade appears to be more limited. Here we investigate the evolution of CTCF as an architectural factor through a cross-species functional replacement approach, in which orthologs from divergent bilaterian lineages are expressed in either mouse embryonic stem cells or Drosophila embryos. Chromosome conformation analyses demonstrated that the ability of CTCF to mediate loop extrusion-dependent TAD formation is encoded within the vertebrate protein. Yet, this property alone is insufficient to establish vertebrate-like genome architecture in an invertebrate background, suggesting the existence of additional lineage-specific factors. ChIP-seq analyses further revealed that CTCF DNA-binding specificity has been progressively refined during bilaterian evolution, with the vertebrate lineage exhibiting a preferential shift towards distal genomic regions that is consistent with an expansion of regulatory domains. Notably, despite failing to form TADs, the ortholog from a non-vertebrate chordate partially rescued the lethality associated with the loss of the vertebrate CTCF protein, indicating that its architectural and regulatory functions are evolutionary independent. Further Hi-C analyses in tunicate and cyclostome species suggest that the ability of CTCF to act as a loop extrusion barrier was already present in the last common ancestor of all vertebrates. Altogether, our findings establish the diversification of CTCF regulation and function as key drivers for the specialization of bilaterian 3D genome organization.

摘要翻译

CTCF是一种DNA结合蛋白，出现于最后一个两侧对称动物祖先中，并在三维基因组组织中发挥核心作用。然而，尽管其重要性，基因组研究已揭示CTCF在不同动物支系中的功能存在显著差异。在脊椎动物中，CTCF通过cohesin介导的环挤压对TAD组织至关重要，而在该支系之外，其对染色质组织的贡献似乎更为有限。在此，我们通过跨物种功能替换方法研究CTCF作为结构因子的进化，其中来自不同两侧对称动物谱系的直系同源物在小鼠胚胎干细胞或果蝇胚胎中表达。染色体构象分析表明，CTCF介导环挤压依赖性TAD形成的能力编码于脊椎动物蛋白中。然而，仅此特性不足以在无脊椎动物背景下建立脊椎动物样基因组结构，提示存在额外的谱系特异性因子。ChIP-seq分析进一步揭示，CTCF的DNA结合特异性在两侧对称动物进化过程中逐步精细化，脊椎动物谱系表现出向远端基因组区域的优先转移，这与调控域扩展一致。值得注意的是，尽管未能形成TAD，来自非脊椎动物脊索动物的直系同源物部分挽救了与脊椎动物CTCF蛋白缺失相关的致死性，表明其结构和调控功能在进化上是独立的。在海鞘和圆口类物种中的进一步Hi-C分析表明，CTCF作为环挤压屏障的能力已存在于所有脊椎动物的最后一个共同祖先中。总体而言，我们的发现确立了CTCF调控和功能的多样化作为两侧对称动物三维基因组组织特化的关键驱动因素。
