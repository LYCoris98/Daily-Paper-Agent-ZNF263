Histone modifications define distinct nucleosome positions in cell-free DNA

Ou Y; Gulati GS; Tyndall CF; Pandey M; Seo J; Zhang Z; Jin Z; Lee GG; Nawfal R; Hoggood TA; Shahbazi SD; Ludwig J; Zhang Z; Ibrahim E; Nafeh G; Berchuck JE; Sowalsky AG; Karzai F; Freedman ML; Takeda DY; Baca SC
bioRxiv 预印本 · 2026-10-28 · 仅摘要 · 原文

该文利用cfDNA核小体定位揭示组蛋白修饰状态，虽未直接研究cpcdh基因家族，但其方法学（cfChIP-seq和MAP分析）可借鉴用于cpcdh基因家族的表观遗传调控研究。

摘要

<h4>ABSTRACT</h4> Circulating cell-free DNA (cfDNA) fragmentation patterns reflect nucleosome positioning. We asked whether variation in nucleosome positions in cfDNA can reveal chromatin states and associated histone modifications. Using cell-free chromatin immunoprecipitation sequencing (cfChIP-seq) in plasma from patients with prostate cancer, we identified 35,120 modification-associated positions (MAPs), loci where nucleosomes bearing active (H3K4me2) and repressive (H3K9me3) histone marks occupy reproducibly offset positions. These offsets recurred across histone marks, an independent cfChIP-seq cohort, and nuclease-digested chromatin from cell lines. MAPs were enriched in transcribed genes but depleted near transcription start sites and CTCF-binding sites where nucleosome positioning is constrained. Across 2,535 cfDNA sequencing datasets, nucleosome protection at a subset of MAPs correlated with tumor fraction (TF), reflecting differences in histone modification status between cancer-derived and non-malignant chromatin. In targeted sequencing panels, a 50-MAP model using fragments from < 0.001% of the genome distinguished samples with tumor fractions ≥3% vs 0% (AUCs = 0.91-0.96 across prostate, breast, and lung cancers) and outperformed a state-of-the-art fragmentomics-based tumor fraction predictor. MAP-derived estimates correlated with reference TFs ( r = 0.89) and longitudinal changes in TF ( r = 0.91). MAP protection at ASCL1 binding sites identified neuroendocrine transformation of prostate cancer from cfDNA. These findings establish histone-modification-associated nucleosome positioning as a mutation-independent readout of tumor fraction and cancer state from routine cfDNA sequencing.

摘要翻译

<h4>摘要</h4> 循环游离DNA（cfDNA）的片段化模式反映了核小体定位。我们探究了cfDNA中核小体位置的变异是否能揭示染色质状态及相关的组蛋白修饰。通过对前列腺癌患者血浆中的游离染色质免疫沉淀测序（cfChIP-seq），我们识别出35,120个修饰相关位置（MAPs），这些位点上携带活性（H3K4me2）和抑制性（H3K9me3）组蛋白标记的核小体占据可重复的偏移位置。这些偏移在组蛋白标记、独立的cfChIP-seq队列以及细胞系中核酸酶消化的染色质中反复出现。MAPs在转录基因中富集，但在转录起始位点和CTCF结合位点附近减少，这些区域的核小体定位受到约束。在2,535个cfDNA测序数据集中，MAPs子集的核小体保护与肿瘤分数（TF）相关，反映了癌症来源与非恶性染色质之间组蛋白修饰状态的差异。在靶向测序面板中，一个使用基因组<0.001%片段的50-MAP模型区分了肿瘤分数≥3%与0%的样本（前列腺癌、乳腺癌和肺癌的AUCs = 0.91-0.96），并优于基于片段组学的先进肿瘤分数预测器。MAP衍生的估计值与参考TF（r = 0.89）及TF的纵向变化（r = 0.91）相关。ASCL1结合位点的MAP保护识别了前列腺癌从cfDNA的神经内分泌转化。这些发现确立了组蛋白修饰相关核小体定位作为从常规cfDNA测序中获取的肿瘤分数和癌症状态的突变独立读数。

06 核心思想

1) 表面方法：用 cfChIP-seq 在血浆中定位 H3K4me2 和 H3K9me3 关联的核小体位置，定义 MAPs，再用 cfDNA 测序数据评估 MAP 处的核小体保护。 2) 核心洞察：组蛋白修饰状态会导致核小体占据位置发生可重复的偏移，这种偏移在 cfDNA 中可被检测，并因 cancer-derived 与 non-malignant chromatin 的修饰差异而与 tumor fraction 相关。 3) 可能的普适教训 [Analysis]：染色质状态可通过核小体定位的「偏移」而非仅「覆盖」来读取；小 panel 若选取信息量高的位点，可达到全基因组 fragmentomics 难以达到的低肿瘤分数区分能力。

Reviewer

  • Overall assessment The abstract describes a conceptually interesting extension of cfDNA fragmentomics, namely that histone-modification state may impose reproducible nucleosome positional offsets detectable in plasma. The reported effect sizes (AUC 0.91-0.96, r = 0.89-0.91) and the breadth of the dataset (2,535 cfDNA datasets) are notable if substantiated. However, from the supplied material alone the central claims are not independently assessable. Key concerns include whether the observed offsets are attributable to histone modification per se rather than to underlying sequence, GC content, or chromatin accessibility; whether the cfChIP-seq signal is specific given the low input and antibody-dependent nature of the assay; and whether the reported performance is established on held-out data. The work is potentially of interest but currently rests on unverifiable abstract-level assertions.
  • Who would be interested in the results, and why Researchers in liquid biopsy and cfDNA fragmentomics, epigenetics and chromatin biology, prostate and genitourinary oncology, and computational genomics. If validated, a mutation-independent, histone-modification-informed readout of tumor fraction and cancer state would be relevant to minimal residual disease monitoring, treatment response tracking, and lineage plasticity such as neuroendocrine transformation.
  • Major strengths
  • Addresses a clear biological question, whether cfDNA nucleosome positioning encodes chromatin state and histone modification identity.
  • Reports replication across histone marks, an independent cfChIP-seq cohort, and nuclease-digested cell-line chromatin, which if properly executed strengthens generalizability.
  • Uses a large external corpus (2,535 cfDNA sequencing datasets) for the TF correlation analysis.
  • Demonstrates a potential translational application with a compact 50-MAP panel and comparison against an existing fragmentomics-based predictor.
  • Includes a disease-state application (ASCL1-linked neuroendocrine transformation) that, if robust, would be clinically meaningful.
