# DNMT1-Mediated Epigenetic Reprogramming Drives
KMT2A
Amplifications and Rearrangements


## STATEMENT OF SIGNIFICANCE
  This study reveals a conserved DNA-histone methylation mechanism controlling the susceptibility of the KMT2A locus to copy gain and rearrange. These events can be generated or prevented by genetic, therapeutic or epigenome editing. Furthermore, DNMT1 inhibition blocks chemotherapy-induced KMT2A disruption, identifying a therapeutic strategy to intercept these oncogenic genomic alterations.
